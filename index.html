Material Roll

Fire the ball from the cannon into the glowing green goal. You set the rotation, the elevation and the power; physics does the rest. Each level is a box built from unit cubes, every block type has its own friction, bounce and behaviour, and every ball material reacts to them differently.

  • IceAlmost no friction.
  • MudGrips and drags.
  • GlassBreaks under enough impact energy.
  • Belts, springsPush or launch the ball.
  • LavaSends the ball back to the cannon.
